Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

This policy covers the personal video publishing tools run by A30Richer. The tools turn videos that the owner produces into YouTube uploads on the owner's own channels. They are not offered as a service to the public.

What the tools access

  • The tools request one Google permission: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload. It lets them upload a video and set its title, description, tags and privacy.
  • They do not read, change or delete existing videos, comments, subscribers, analytics or any other channel data, and they do not access Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google service.

How Google user data is used

  • The OAuth tokens that Google issues are used only to upload videos to the channel of the account that granted access.
  • Tokens are stored on infrastructure controlled by the owner (the owner's own device and private CI secrets). They are never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • No personal data of other people is collected through Google sign-in.

Limited Use

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and removal

Tokens are kept only while uploads are needed. Access can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party connections, after which the stored token stops working and is deleted.
